The One Rule
The habit is not the point. Becoming the woman who does what she says in the moment — consistently, even when she doesn't feel like it — changes everything. Use the habits as the tool. The identity is the goal.
Phase 1
Build the Foundation
Days 1–14 · 2 habits · 14 days straight
Pick two things that seem simple but you can't do consistently. Not big goals — small daily overrides. The kind of thing your brain will tell you is "too easy to matter." That thought is Akrasia. Don't listen to it.

Why Phase 1 Works
This is behavioral conditioning. Every time you close the gap between "I should" and "I did," you reinforce a neural pathway — training your brain to associate intention with action. You're not building the habit. You're building the identity of someone who keeps her word to herself.
Phase 2
Add Weight
Days 15–21 · 3 habits · Keep Phase 1 going
You have 14 days of evidence now. Add one more thing — slightly harder than the first two. Something that requires a bit more override. Keep doing your Phase 1 habits.

What Happens Next
Repeat this framework every month. Add slightly harder things each cycle. Month 1 you build the foundation. Month 3 you're doing things month-1-you couldn't have started. The growth compounds — like building muscle. By month six the distance between who you were and who you are is almost unrecognizable.
